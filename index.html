Quick Pre-Ride Safety Checklist

  1. Check front and rear tyre pressure weekly.
  2. Test both brake levers before moving out.
  3. Look under engine for any oil drops.
  4. Check chain tightness and lube after rain.
  5. Verify headlight, brake light and indicators.
